# NexusAIAgency premium website

## Build
- Create a polished multi-page agency site with a shared sticky navigation, mobile menu, dark/light theme switcher, and responsive footer.
- Use the requested deep-navy enterprise style with electric mint accents, subtle grid texture, sharp typography, restrained motion, and accessible contrast.
- Use the uploaded founder portrait and certificate as authentic visual proof.

## Pages
- Home: hero, interactive readiness audit, business pain points, AI workforce, connected-system flow, demo lab, industries, four-step process, offers, proof, booking callout, and FAQ.
- Solutions: detailed AI Support, Sales, Calling, Research, Operations, CRM, RPM, and data-management systems.
- Industries: real estate alongside clinics, gyms, coaching, institutes, shops, digital businesses, agencies, e-commerce, B2B, SaaS, recruitment, and education.
- Agent Demos: interactive previews for website support, lead qualification, calling, research, and CRM automation.
- How It Works: Audit, Design, Deploy, Optimize, with clear expectations and deliverables.
- About: founder profile, certificate, philosophy, and supplied social channels.
- Book Audit: qualification and booking form with direct phone, WhatsApp, and email options.
- Privacy: clear privacy policy for contact and audit submissions.

## Forms and data
- Add a shared lead-capture database with row-level protection; visitors can submit but cannot read stored leads.
- Build and validate the readiness audit, demo request, and booking forms on both the page and server.
- Store every submission with its source and timestamp, then show a clear confirmation.
- Verify form delivery with test submissions and remove only the test records afterward.

## Technical details
- Use the project’s TanStack Start architecture, TypeScript, Tailwind v4 tokens, Zod validation, and Lovable Cloud.
- Keep every menu destination as a separate route with unique search and social metadata.
- Add a persistent theme preference and honor the visitor’s system preference on first visit.
- Use semantic design tokens rather than hard-coded page colors, and preserve keyboard/focus accessibility.
